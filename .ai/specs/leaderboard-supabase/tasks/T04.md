## Exact values (Ablehnungsgründe)

`session_unknown` 403, `session_used` 409, `session_expired` 410, `seed_mismatch` / `map_mismatch` / `mode_mismatch` / `version_mismatch` 422,
`replay_invalid` 400 (Text aus `parseReplay`), `too_fast` 422 (`elapsed_ms < endTick / 60 / 10 × 1000`), `duplicate` 409.
